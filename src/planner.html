Spreadsheet: click / drag / Shift+click or Shift+arrows to select, type or Enter / F2 / double-click to edit, Enter / Tab to move on. Ctrl+Enter puts the value in every selected cell. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste with Excel or Sheets (rows pasted past the end become new waypoints); Delete clears speeds; Ctrl+A selects all.

Click: add waypoint (on the line: insert). Drag a waypoint: move it. Right-click: delete. Wheel over a waypoint: height; Shift+wheel: speed. Elsewhere drag = pan, wheel = zoom. Touch: tap = add, long-press a waypoint = delete, pinch = zoom, tap a cell to edit it. Cyan = predicted path, a tick every minute. Maps appear after one flight on them with the mod, airframes after one flight in them.
